The 50–60 kg jute gunny sack is the most widely deployed packaging format in Indian and global agricultural trade, and for good reason: its combination of manageable size, robust construction and low unit cost makes it the natural choice for a vast range of commodities from paddy and wheat to onions, potatoes and fertilizer granules. Our JSB-0003 is built to this proven specification with the material integrity and stitch quality that buyers in both domestic FCI-approved procurement and international export markets have come to expect from a serious manufacturer.

The plain-sewn construction on this model is entirely appropriate for the load range: for 50–60 kg applications the stitch strength is more than adequate, and the simpler construction allows us to deliver at extremely competitive unit pricing without compromising on jute quality or weave density. The optional blue stripe variant is available for buyers whose customers use stripe colour to differentiate commodity types at warehouse intake.

This size range is also the most popular choice for buyers who want to combine multiple commodity uses from a single SKU — the same bag that stores wheat in a Punjab warehouse ships potatoes out of Agra and handles cement in a construction supply chain. If your operation spans multiple commodities, the JSB-0003 is the economical way to standardise your packaging inventory.
