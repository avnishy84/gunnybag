India is the world's largest processor and exporter of cashew nuts, and the packaging used for export plays a direct role in the premium the commodity commands at destination. Our JSB-0004 jute gunny sacks for cashew and tree nuts are manufactured to the exacting standards of Cashew Export Promotion Council of India (CEPC) recommendations and international food-import compliance requirements in the EU, USA and Gulf markets.

The breathable open-weave construction of natural jute is ideally suited to cashew storage because it allows the slow equilibration of moisture content that keeps kernels crisp without promoting the humidity spikes that cause mould or aflatoxin contamination. Unlike synthetic woven polypropylene alternatives, our jute sacks do not generate static charge, do not leach plasticisers into the food product, and are fully biodegradable at end of life — a growing requirement in EU and UK import documentation.

We supply these bags to processors and exporters in Kollam, Mangalore, Goa, Daman and Mumbai — India's primary cashew processing corridor — and have established supply relationships with nut exporters sourcing product from West Africa, Vietnam and Brazil for repackaging and re-export through Indian ports. Our quality control team samples every production batch against a standard reference sack maintained in our testing laboratory to ensure dimensional consistency and stitch count compliance.
