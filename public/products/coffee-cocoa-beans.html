Export Packing: 300 bags / export-standard iron-bound bale. Iron-bound bales designed for standard container stuffing. Phytosanitary and fumigation certificates available on request.

Coffee and cocoa beans are hygroscopic commodities — they absorb and release moisture continuously after harvest, and the packaging they travel in directly determines whether they arrive at destination as a premium crop or a compromised one. Our jute gunny bags for coffee and cocoa are woven to a tighter specification that balances two competing needs: sufficient breathability to allow off-gassing and prevent condensation build-up, and enough structural integrity to survive the multi-modal supply chains that connect tropical origin countries with roasters in Europe, North America and East Asia.

The food-grade natural jute fibre we use for these bags is free of synthetic coatings, bleaching agents and chemical preservatives, making them fully compliant with food-contact material regulations across major importing markets including the EU, UK, USA and Japan. The blue centre stripe is widely recognised in the commodity trade as the standard identifier for coffee-grade jute sacks, ensuring your shipment is handled according to food-commodity protocols at every port stop.

We supply these bags to exporters across Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu and the North-East, as well as to trading houses exporting Ethiopian, Ugandan and Vietnamese origin beans transshipped through Indian ports. Phytosanitary treatment and fumigation certificates can be arranged prior to container loading on request.
