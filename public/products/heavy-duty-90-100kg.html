Our Heavy Duty Jute Gunny Sacks are purpose-engineered for the most demanding bulk storage and transportation applications. Woven from premium-grade raw jute fibres sourced directly from certified farms in West Bengal and Bangladesh, each sack undergoes rigorous tensile-strength testing to guarantee it holds its rated load under real-world export and logistics conditions.

The HKL-sewn hem provides a double-lock stitch at every seam, virtually eliminating seam failure during mechanical handling and stacking. The distinctive green centre stripe is not merely cosmetic — it serves as a quick visual identifier during warehouse sorting, reducing handling errors and mix-ups in busy logistics environments.

Every bale is packed to export-standard dimensions and iron-bound for container stuffing efficiency. With an industry-leading bale density of 400 bags per bale, our heavy-duty sacks cut your per-unit freight cost while maintaining the structural integrity demanded by port handling equipment and long ocean transits.
