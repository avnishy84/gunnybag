About Our Company

An established Indian manufacturer and exporter of 100% natural jute gunny bags, supplying buyers across six continents with consistent quality and reliable logistics.

Our Story

We began as a small weaving operation in the jute-growing heartland of Eastern India, supplying domestic grain markets with standard-specification gunny sacks. Over decades of steady growth, we developed our own production facility — adding weaving looms, stitching lines and a dedicated quality lab under one roof — which gave us both the capacity to serve large export volumes and the control to maintain consistent specifications across every production run.

The shift to export markets came naturally: buyers in Europe, the Middle East, Africa and North America were seeking a reliable Indian source of food-grade and heavy-duty jute sacks that could meet international documentation requirements without the unpredictability of trading-house supply chains. We invested in our export capability — from iron-bound baling equipment and phytosanitary treatment facilities through to dedicated shipping and documentation teams — and built our reputation order by order through on-time delivery and transparent communication.

Today, our product range covers the full spectrum of jute sack applications: heavy-duty bulk cargo, food-grade coffee and cocoa storage, general-purpose grain and produce packaging, and nut and cashew export. All four product lines are manufactured in our own facility, from raw jute to finished bale, so we can answer for the quality of every unit we ship.

Quality Control

Quality at our facility is not an end-of-line inspection — it is built into every stage of production. Raw jute is graded on arrival and substandard fibre rejected before it reaches the looms. Weave density and weight are measured per-roll during weaving, not just on finished bags. Stitching operators are trained to a minimum stitch count and seam strength standard, and random samples from every production run are tensile-tested in our lab before the batch is released for baling.

For food-grade products, our handling and production areas are maintained to food-contact material standards. No chemical finishing agents or synthetic coatings are used on food-grade lines, and all raw materials are traceable to source. We retain production records and batch certificates for a minimum of three years, available to buyers on request.

  • Incoming raw jute grading and rejection protocol
  • In-line weave density and weight checks during production
  • Stitch count and seam strength verification per batch
  • Dimensional inspection on finished bags before baling
  • Pre-shipment inspection (PSI) facilitated with approved third-party inspectors on request

Sustainability & Eco Credentials

Jute is naturally sustainable — we work to keep it that way across our entire operation.

🌱

100% Natural Fibre

Jute is a rain-fed crop that requires minimal pesticide and no artificial irrigation. Our bags contain no synthetic fibres, coatings or plastic components.

♻️

Fully Biodegradable

Natural jute decomposes completely within months under composting conditions. No microplastic residue, no long-term soil contamination.

💨

Low Carbon Crop

Jute plants sequester CO₂ at approximately 15 tonnes per hectare per season — higher than most other commercial fibre crops. Our supply chain starts with a net-positive crop.

🤝

Rural Employment

Our manufacturing creates skilled and semi-skilled employment within jute-growing communities, contributing directly to rural livelihoods in Eastern India.
